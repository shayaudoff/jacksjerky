Who we are

The Story Behind
Jack's Jerky

At Jack’s Jerky, We Don’t Just Make Beef Jerky—We Craft a Legacy.

For over half a century, our family has been perfecting the art of jerky, starting in a small kitchen where our grandfather first seasoned the meat. Passed down from generation to generation, every step of our process is done with care and tradition. Today, Jack’s Jerky stands as a symbol of quality, handcrafted flavor, and family pride.

We believe that the best jerky is more than just a snack—it’s a companion for life’s moments. Whether you’re out on a hike, heading to a game, or just kicking back with the boys, Jack’s Jerky is by your side.

And because we care about every customer, every batch of our jerky is 100% kosher, ensuring that it meats (typo intended) the highest standards of quality and tradition.

Our team is more than employees—they’re family. They bring that same dedication and passion, ensuring every piece of jerky is hand-crafted, full of flavor, and always done better.

At Jack’s Jerky, we don’t just make beef jerky—we build memories, one bite at a time.
